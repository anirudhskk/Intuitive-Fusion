## In scope

### FR-0110 · Must
RFx/tender documents (requirements, deliverables, scope) shall be auto-populated from the intake conversation.

**Acceptance:** Given a completed intake conversation, when the RFx is opened, then requirements, deliverables and scope sections are pre-populated from that conversation and remain fully editable.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0115 · Should
Users shall be able to drag-and-drop to design the RFx/tender template layout, or use a system-provided default template.

**Acceptance:** Given the RFx layout designer, when a user arranges and sizes fields, then the layout is applied to the published tender pack; where no custom layout exists, the system default template is used.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0120 · Must
Completed RFx collaboration content shall automatically compile, via a tender package assembler, into a single published tender pack bundled with corporate terms and legal conditions.

**Acceptance:** Given finalised RFx content, when the tender pack is generated, then a single bundled pack is produced containing the RFx content plus the tenant's corporate terms and legal conditions, with no manual assembly step.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0125 · Should
The system shall support a structured legal deviation register, capturing supplier-proposed contract deviations as discrete fields, exportable to Word/Excel, with legal commentary captured against each item and re-importable for onward supplier response.

**Acceptance:** Given supplier-proposed deviations, when the register is exported to Word or Excel, annotated by legal and re-imported, then each deviation retains its identity and the added legal commentary is attached to the correct item.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0135 · Must
The system shall provide an anonymised Q&A clarification channel: vendor questions are captured without identifying the source, and buyer responses are broadcast as a unified addendum to all registered bidders.

**Acceptance:** Given a question submitted by a registered bidder, when the buyer publishes a response as an addendum, then the addendum is issued to all registered bidders and the questioner's identity is not disclosed in the published content.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0140 · Must
The platform shall route the finalised tender notice automatically to mandatory public registers (e.g. AusTender, SAM.gov, TED) for public-sector tenders based on jurisdiction, or to pre-vetted preferred-vendor/MSA-holder panels for private-sector tenders.

**Acceptance:** Given a public-sector tenant and a configured jurisdiction, when a tender is published, then the notice is lodged with the applicable statutory register and the lodgement reference is recorded; for private-sector tenants, the notice is issued to the nominated panel.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0145 · Must
For public-sector tenders, the system shall validate that the gap between publication and closing dates meets applicable statutory minimums (e.g. a 25-day window under WTO Government Procurement rules) before allowing publication.

**Acceptance:** Given a public-sector tender whose publication-to-close window is shorter than the configured statutory minimum, when publication is attempted, then publication is blocked and the shortfall is reported to the user.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0150 · Must
The tender shall remain in a locked, unreleased 'staged' state until a delegate 'permission to publish' action, routed by the plan's ECV, is recorded.

**Acceptance:** Given a staged tender, when no permission-to-publish decision has been recorded by the ECV-appropriate delegate, then the tender cannot be released and is not visible to any supplier.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.
