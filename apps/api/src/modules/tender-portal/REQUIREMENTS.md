## In scope

### FR-0155 · Must
A supplier/tenderer shall be able to view only one procurement (tender) at a time within the portal, and shall be able to download key tender information.

**Acceptance:** Given a supplier invited to two tenders, when they are working within one tender, then no content from the other tender is visible, and key tender documents can be downloaded from the active tender.

### FR-0160 · Must
Access shall be controlled per tender: closed tenders shall be invite-only; open tenders shall be accessible to any registered supplier.

**Acceptance:** Given a closed tender, when a registered but uninvited supplier attempts access, then access is refused; given an open tender, then any registered supplier can access it.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0165 · Must
The portal shall enforce defined tender opening and closing times, automatically locking submissions out after close, discarding incomplete transfers, and issuing an automated notification that a late submission was received too late.

**Acceptance:** Given a submission in progress at the closing time, when the deadline passes, then the transfer is discarded, the submission is not accepted, and the supplier receives an automated late-submission notification.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0170 · Must
Suppliers shall be able to upload documents in PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and image formats; submissions shall be encrypted at the point of upload and shall not be viewable or openable by any internal user, including administrators, before the closing deadline passes.

**Acceptance:** Given an uploaded submission before close, when any internal user — including a platform administrator — attempts to open it, then access is refused and the attempt is logged; after close, authorised users can open it.

### FR-0195 · Must
Questions and answers raised through the portal shall be captured and publishable either to a single supplier or broadcast to all suppliers on the tender.

**Acceptance:** Given a supplier question, when the buyer selects single-supplier publication, then only that supplier sees the answer; when broadcast is selected, then all registered suppliers on the tender receive it.

### FR-0200 · Must
The portal shall automatically send email invitations to suppliers invited to a specific tender, and shall automatically generate and release the tender pack from the completed RFx collaboration content.

**Acceptance:** Given a supplier added to a tender invitation list, when the tender is released, then an invitation email is sent automatically and the generated tender pack is available to that supplier in the portal.

### FR-0205 · Should
Procurement or administrator roles shall be able to grant permission for a nominated supplier to submit after the closing time where there is a justified reason, reopening the tender for that supplier only, with the decision and justification logged.

**Acceptance:** Given a closed tender, when an authorised administrator grants a late-submission exception for a named supplier with a recorded justification, then only that supplier can submit, and the exception is visible in the audit trail.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0210 · Must
Procurement or administrator roles shall be able to issue addenda and change tender dates and times, with automatic notification to all registered bidders.

**Acceptance:** Given a released tender, when an administrator issues an addendum or amends the closing date/time, then all registered bidders are notified automatically and the change is recorded in the tender history.

### FR-0220 · Should
Multi-stage tendering shall support a separate document set, submission round and report per stage, including second and third stage submissions from shortlisted suppliers (e.g. post-demonstration submissions, negotiation or BAFO rounds).

**Acceptance:** Given a two-stage tender, when stage 1 shortlisting completes, then a distinct stage 2 document set and submission round are opened to shortlisted suppliers only, and each stage retains its own submissions and report.

### FR-0225 · Must
Suppliers who are not shortlisted shall be sent an automated unsuccessful notification once the procurement team or administrator confirms the shortlist outcome.

**Acceptance:** Given a confirmed shortlist, when the confirmation is recorded, then each non-shortlisted supplier receives an automated unsuccessful notification and the despatch is logged.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0230 · Must
Where a supplier submits across multiple stages, the AI shall treat the latest submission version as authoritative while capturing content from earlier versions that has not been superseded, for inclusion in the contract.

**Acceptance:** Given successive submissions from one supplier, when the contract is drafted, then superseded content is excluded, non-superseded content from earlier versions is retained, and the provenance of each retained item is traceable.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.
