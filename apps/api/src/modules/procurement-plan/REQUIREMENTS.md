## In scope

### FR-0075 · Must
The procurement plan shall be auto-populated from the request intake conversation, with any field or sections within a field amendable by voice or typed input.

**Acceptance:** Given a submitted request, when the procurement plan is opened, then plan fields are pre-populated from the intake conversation and every field can be amended by voice or typed input.

### FR-0080 · Must
The procurement plan shall be viewable and actionable for delegate review and approval on both desktop and mobile devices.

**Acceptance:** Given a delegate on a mobile device, when they open an assigned procurement plan, then they can review all plan content and record an approval or rejection decision without switching to desktop.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

**Acceptance:** Given an administrator in the layout designer, when they drag fields into position and resize them, then the saved layout governs both the on-screen plan and the generated printable document.
