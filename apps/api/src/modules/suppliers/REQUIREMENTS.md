## In scope

### FR-0215 · Should
Enterprises shall be able to configure additional supplier onboarding questions (e.g. modern slavery and sustainability questions) and, where a question set is uploaded as a document, the AI shall ingest and break it into individual questions with an appropriate answer method.

**Acceptance:** Given an uploaded question document, when it is ingested, then discrete questions are created with a selected answer type (e.g. checkbox, free text) and are presented to suppliers during onboarding.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0235 · Must
Suppliers shall be able to self-register by voice or typed input, capturing name, email, company registration number (e.g. ABN), bank account information and other required onboarding fields, with secure upload of supporting company documentation.

**Acceptance:** Given a prospective supplier, when they complete self-registration by voice or typing, then a supplier record is created with all mandatory onboarding fields and uploaded documentation stored against it.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0240 · Must
The system shall send an invitation email to prospective suppliers containing a unique link for the supplier's contact to set up their own portal instance, including privacy settings and login credentials.

**Acceptance:** Given an invited supplier contact, when they follow the unique link, then they can establish their own portal access including privacy settings, and the link cannot be reused by another party.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0245 · Must
Suppliers shall be able to manage and change their registered contacts, including reassigning access when a contact leaves the organisation, with a notification email issued to prompt an update to the supplier profile.

**Acceptance:** Given a supplier contact who has left, when the supplier reassigns that contact's access, then the departing contact's access is revoked, the incoming contact is provisioned, and a profile-update prompt is issued.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0250 · Must
The supplier profile shall carry current insurance certificate and sanctions-screening status, shared with the Tender Portal onboarding checks.

**Acceptance:** Given a supplier profile, when it is viewed by an authorised internal user, then current insurance certificate status and the latest sanctions-screening result and date are displayed, and the same data drives the Tender Portal checks.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.
