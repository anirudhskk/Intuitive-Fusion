## In scope

### FR-0380 · Must
The final, approved contract version shall route automatically into a contract-signing workflow, with a configurable review/approval chain prior to signing.

**Acceptance:** Given an approved final contract version, when it is released for signature, then it enters the signing workflow automatically and each configured review/approval step must complete in order before signature is enabled.

### FR-0385 · Must
Where a customer has no external legal system, the platform shall provide native legal matter management, comprising a legal kanban board, review-hour logging, and a clause library of corporate fallback positions and mandatory boilerplate.

**Acceptance:** Given a tenant without an external legal system, when a matter is raised, then it appears on the legal kanban board, review hours can be logged against it, and clauses can be inserted from the clause library.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0395 · Must
The system shall provide a dynamic clause assembly engine that constructs a customised contract draft from project metadata (value, sector, risk tier, governing region), injecting mandatory government boilerplate for public-sector projects and appropriate IP protections for technology purchases.

**Acceptance:** Given a public-sector technology procurement, when the draft is assembled, then mandatory government boilerplate and IP protection clauses are present, and the metadata that selected each clause is recorded.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0400 · Must
The system shall monitor the negotiation room for material deviations to non-negotiable mandatory clauses (e.g. indemnity limits, liability caps, IP governance); a detected deviation shall block signature routing and force an approval loop to General Counsel or the risk delegate.

**Acceptance:** Given a material deviation to a mandatory clause, when signature routing is attempted, then routing is blocked and an approval task is raised to General Counsel or the risk delegate, and only their approval releases the block.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0405 · Must
During contract drafting, the system shall cross-check the winning supplier's tender submission against the original RFx and flag any inconsistencies (e.g. pricing, scope or terms that differ from what was tendered) for legal/procurement review before the draft proceeds.

**Acceptance:** Given a winning submission that differs from the RFx on price, scope or terms, when the draft is assembled, then each inconsistency is itemised for review and the draft cannot proceed until every item is accepted or resolved.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0415 · Must
The system shall run a pre-flight verification of the vendor's legal name, tax registration and banking details against ERP/vetted records before signature options unlock.

**Acceptance:** Given a vendor whose banking or tax details do not match the ERP/vetted record, when signature is requested, then signature options remain locked and the mismatch is reported for resolution.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0420 · Must
The platform shall provide a native, eIDAS-aligned electronic/digital signature capability as a baseline, with interim or ongoing integration to third-party e-signature providers (e.g. DocuSign, Adobe Sign) supporting concurrent (any order) and sequential (defined order) multi-party signing.

**Acceptance:** Given a contract requiring three signatories, when concurrent signing is selected, then all three can sign in any order; when sequential signing is selected, then each signatory is only invited after the preceding signature is recorded.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0425 · Should
The platform shall support advanced signing workflows, including blind signing (a signatory signs without other parties' signatures or identities visible) and staged endorsement (signatures collected in a defined sequence of approval stages), with preset signature-block placement configurable within document templates.

**Acceptance:** Given a template with preset signature blocks, when content is added and the document length changes, then the signature blocks remain correctly placed; and in blind signing, a signatory cannot see other parties' signatures or identities.

> ⚠ Open client query — see register Notes column.

### FR-0445 · Should

**Acceptance:** Given a signature request outstanding beyond the configured reminder interval, when the interval elapses, then a reminder is issued; and a signatory can view the full contract and raise a question against it before signing.

### FR-0455 · Must
Once signed, a contract shall be locked; deletion shall be restricted to authorised roles and shall always be logical (soft) rather than permanent, with the underlying record retained permanently in storage and recoverable.

**Acceptance:** Given a signed contract, when an authorised role deletes it, then it is removed from the tenant's active view but the record is retained, recoverable and evidenced in the audit trail; unauthorised roles cannot delete it.
